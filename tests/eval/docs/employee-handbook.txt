NIMBUS TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. Working Hours
Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Employees may start any time between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
Fridays are remote-work days for all teams.

2. Leave Policy
Every employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 8 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year.
Sick leave is 10 days per year and cannot be carried forward.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
Leave longer than 5 consecutive days must be requested at least two weeks in advance.

3. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published every December.

4. Probation
New employees are on probation for 3 months. During probation, annual leave is limited to 2 days.
