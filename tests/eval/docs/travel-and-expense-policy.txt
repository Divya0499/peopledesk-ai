NIMBUS TECHNOLOGIES - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

1. Booking
All business travel must be booked through the Orbit travel portal at least 10 days before the trip.
Flights must be economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours.

2. Daily Allowance
Employees receive a daily meal allowance of 75 US dollars for international travel and 2,000 rupees for domestic travel.

3. Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at 180 US dollars per night. A director must approve any exception.

4. Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from the trip, with original receipts.
Claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

5. Not Covered
Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursed.
